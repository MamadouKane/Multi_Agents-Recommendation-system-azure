## Which payment methods do you accept?
In the café we accept credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash. Orders placed with
the assistant for delivery are paid by card.

## Do you have wifi?
Yes, the café has free wifi. Ask the staff for the password.

## Do you offer plant-based milk?
Not at the moment. Our milk drinks are made with dairy milk. The drinks without milk are listed
in our dairy-free information.

## Do you have gluten-free products?
Not at the moment. Every bakery item on our menu is made with wheat flour or oats. Coffee, drinking
chocolate and syrups contain no gluten ingredient.

## Can you guarantee that a product is free from an allergen?
No. All our products are prepared in the same kitchen, so traces of milk, eggs, gluten, nuts and
soy are possible in any item. If you have a severe allergy, please speak to a member of staff
before ordering.

## Can I place a large order for a group or an event?
Yes. For orders of more than twenty items, please order at least 24 hours in advance so we can
prepare everything fresh.

## Can I customise my drink?
Yes. You can add any of our flavour syrups to a coffee. Ask for the syrups on the menu.

## Do you roast your own coffee?
Yes. Our beans come from small farms and cooperatives in South America and are roasted in-house.

## How do I order?
You can order in the café, or through the assistant for delivery within Greenwich Village, SoHo,
West Village and Lower Manhattan.

## Can I cancel or change an order?
You can change or cancel an order with the assistant until you confirm it. Once it is confirmed,
please contact the café directly.
