Merry's Way Coffee is a neighbourhood coffee shop in the heart of Greenwich Village, New York City.
It was founded in 2015 as a small family-owned café with one mission: to share quality,
ethically sourced coffee with the community.

Merry's passion for travel and coffee took her across South America, where she built partnerships
with small farms and cooperatives. The beans are roasted in-house to bring out the flavours of the
regions where they were grown.
