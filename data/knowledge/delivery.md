Merry's Way delivers to four neighbourhoods of Manhattan: Greenwich Village, SoHo, West Village
and Lower Manhattan. Deliveries reach homes, offices and Washington Square Park.

Addresses outside these four neighbourhoods are not served by delivery, but everyone is welcome
in the café.
